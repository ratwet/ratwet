### 🤖 AIML Intern

**ShadowFox — Remote**

`June 2025`

Worked on three end-to-end AI/ML tasks spanning model development, analytics, evaluation, and documentation.

#### CIFAR-10 CNN

* Built a convolutional neural network **from scratch without pretrained weights**.
* Classified CIFAR-10 images into **10 categories**.
* Achieved **74.07% test accuracy** after **25 training epochs** using GPU acceleration.

#### Retail Analytics

* Cleaned and analyzed **8,399 retail orders** representing approximately **$14.9M in sales**.
* Used **Pandas** and **Plotly** for data cleaning, analysis, and visualization.
* Found that **Small Business** customers delivered the highest profit margin at **11.32%**, despite having the lowest total sales among the segments.

#### LLM Evaluation & Interpretability

* Deployed and evaluated **Google Gemma 2 (2B)** on GPU across **five task types**, including reasoning, summarization, and creative writing.
* Benchmarked observations against a **GPT-2 baseline**.
* Visualized attention weights to investigate model behavior and interpretability.
